Karachi - City of Lights

The Vibrant Heart of Pakistan

About Karachi

Karachi is the largest city in Pakistan and the capital of Sindh province. Known as the "City of Lights," it is the country's financial, commercial, and industrial hub. Located on the Arabian Sea, Karachi is a bustling metropolis that blends modernity with rich cultural heritage.

Top Tourist Attractions

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach is one of Karachi's most popular tourist destinations. Stretching along the Arabian Sea, the beach offers stunning views of the sunset, camel rides, and a variety of local street food.

Mohatta Palace

Mohatta Palace is a stunning example of Indo-Saracenic architecture, built in 1927. Today, it houses a museum showcasing Pakistani art, culture, and history.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

The Pakistan Maritime Museum showcases the country's naval history and maritime heritage. The museum features an impressive collection of naval artifacts, including a real submarine that visitors can explore.
