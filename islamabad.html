Islamabad – The Beautiful Capital

Nestled against the scenic Margalla Hills, Islamabad is Pakistan's meticulously planned capital city. Known for its greenery, wide boulevards, and modern architecture, it offers a serene blend of nature and urban life.

Top Tourist Attractions in Islamabad

Faisal Mosque

Designed by Turkish architect Vedat Dalokay, the Faisal Mosque sits at the foot of the Margalla Hills. Its unique contemporary design and massive prayer hall make it a must‑visit landmark.

Pakistan Monument

Located in the Shakarparian Hills, this beautiful flower‑shaped monument offers a panoramic view of the city. The adjacent museum provides a deep dive into Pakistan's rich history.

Daman‑e‑Koh

Just a short drive up the Margalla Hills, Daman‑e‑Koh is the perfect spot to watch the sunset over the city. It features lush greenery and a small restaurant for visitors.

Rawal Lake

An artificial reservoir that provides water to the twin cities of Rawalpindi and Islamabad. The lake is surrounded by a beautiful park, making it an ideal weekend getaway for families.
