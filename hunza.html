Hunza Valley

The Jewel of Gilgit-Baltistan

About Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley is a mountainous valley in the Gilgit-Baltistan region of Pakistan. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, the valley is surrounded by the Karakoram mountain range, including the famous Rakaposhi peak. The valley is also home to the ancient Baltit Fort, which stands as a testament to the region's rich history and culture.

Top Tourist Attractions

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

Rakaposhi is one of the most prominent peaks in the Karakoram range, standing at 7,788 meters. The viewpoint in Hunza offers a spectacular panoramic view of this majestic mountain, especially during sunrise and sunset when the peak glows in golden hues.

Attabad Lake

Attabad Lake was formed in 2010 after a massive landslide blocked the Hunza River. The lake's striking turquoise waters surrounded by rugged mountains have made it a popular tourist destination. Visitors can enjoy boating, jet skiing, and fishing in its pristine waters.

Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort is a historical fort situated in the Hunza Valley. Dating back over 700 years, the fort served as the residence of the Mirs of Hunza. Today, it houses a museum displaying artifacts, traditional clothing, and historical documents that tell the story of the region's royal heritage.
