Lahore
City of Gardens

Pakistan's Cultural Heart

Lahore is one of Pakistan's most historic and vibrant cities. Known for its Mughal architecture, rich traditions, delicious food, and lively atmosphere, Lahore attracts visitors from around the world who wish to experience its unique heritage.

Badshahi Mosque

Built during the Mughal era by Emperor Aurangzeb, the Badshahi Mosque is one of the largest mosques in the world and a symbol of Lahore's magnificent architectural heritage.

Lahore Fort

The Lahore Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring beautiful palaces, halls, and gardens that showcase the glory of Mughal craftsmanship and history.

Shalimar Gardens

The Shalimar Gardens are among the finest examples of Mughal landscape design. Their fountains, terraces, and greenery make them one of Lahore's most famous tourist attractions.
